Replays the watch's recorded order books (live.db) with the same decision code the watch runs, and follows each settled match's first flagged bet to settlement ($1 per contract if the side won; cost includes Kalshi fees). Nothing is placed: these are paper trades. Two policies: the one each watch was running when the bet was flagged, and the newest watch's throughout. The bankroll here only sizes the bets (each bet is sized on the same bankroll, not compounded); the curve adds up their P&L in settlement order.

± is one standard error across bets (ROI: ratio estimator; hit rate: binomial). "Luck alone" is the spread of the P&L if the policy's own win probabilities were exactly right: an actual P&L inside expected ± 2× that tells you nothing yet.

Sell rules: the newest policy's entries, then at every later poll the cheapest exit (sell the held contracts, or buy the other side), worth 1 − price − fee per contract. Same numbers as scripts/paper_replay.py pnl and sell.
